### Opening the unsigned macOS Desktop App

Job Harness is free, open-source software distributed without an Apple Developer ID signature. On first launch on macOS:

1. Unzip `Job-Harness-v0.4.1-aarch64-apple-darwin.app.zip` and drag **Job Harness.app** into `/Applications`.
2. Right-click **Job Harness.app** → **Open** (or run once in Terminal: `xattr -d com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Job Harness.app"`).
3. On first launch, click **Try with sample data** to explore a pre-filled 10-role workspace with CVs, interview briefs, Kanban, and Sankey immediately — no terminal or personal data required.

## Highlights

- **`jh serve` always requires a bearer token**, even on loopback, so another user on the machine can't call your port. Re-run `jh service install` after updating: it creates `~/.config/job-harness/serve-token` and adds it to the unit. Open the desktop app with `--token-file` (`jh open --ssh ... --token-file PATH`).
- **Remote mode for agents:** `jh configure --remote URL --token-file PATH` makes `jh call` and `jh mcp` send every call to a `jh serve`, so an agent can run as a user with no access to the ledger's files. `save_artifact` and `read_artifact` let it hand over a job ad or answers and fetch the frozen CV to upload. See [docs/LINUX-SERVER.md](https://github.com/machineyearningengineer/job-harness/blob/main/docs/LINUX-SERVER.md#agents-without-file-access-remote-mode).
- **Job terms checked at preparation:** `prepare_application` needs the salary, location, work arrangement and office days read from the ad (`"unknown"` allowed), and refuses known values outside `discovery.min_base_salary`, `allowed_locations`, `allowed_work_arrangements` or `max_office_days_per_week`. Reservations made under 0.4.0 must be prepared again.
